About T.O.E

Helping people see more. Helping businesses be seen.

Through Our Eyes is a publication and advertising brand connecting people to what Nigeria has to offer, while helping businesses reach the people who need them.

Why we exist

Nigeria is full of businesses, services, events and opportunities. Many remain unseen because people simply do not know they exist.

Our mission

To make businesses, services, experiences and opportunities easier to discover while providing brands with meaningful visibility and access to their target audiences, one state at a time.

Our vision

To become a trusted discovery and media network connecting people, businesses and opportunities across Nigeria.
