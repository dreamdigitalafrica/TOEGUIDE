      Through Our Eyes (T.O.E.)

      Privacy Policy.

      T.O.E. respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our website, digital platforms, business directory, magazine activities, advertising services and Service Network.

      Effective Date: 11th September, 2026   |   Last Updated: 11th September, 2026

      • 1. Information We Collect

        Depending on how you interact with T.O.E., we may collect:

        • Name and photograph;
        • Phone number, WhatsApp number and email;
        • Business name and business information;
        • Business or service location;
        • Website and social-media details;
        • Products and services offered;
        • Photographs, videos, reviews and other submitted content;
        • Service-provider registration and verification information;
        • References or guarantor information where applicable;
        • Payment and transaction information;
        • Website, device, browser and IP information;
        • QR-code and campaign engagement data.

        We will not publicly display sensitive verification information or private residential details of service providers unless there is a lawful basis to do so.

      • 2. How We Collect Information

        We may collect information when you visit or use our website, submit a business listing, register as a service provider, purchase a T.O.E. service, contact us, subscribe to communications, submit a review or other content, scan a T.O.E. QR code, participate in a T.O.E. event or campaign, or interact with our social-media platforms.

        We may also obtain limited business information from publicly available sources where lawful and appropriate.

      • 3. How We Use Information

        We may use personal information to operate and improve the Platform, create and manage business listings, connect users with businesses and service providers, verify information, provide requested services, process payments, manage advertising and subscriptions, respond to enquiries, send relevant communications, measure QR-code and campaign performance, produce reports and analytics, investigate complaints and maintain Platform safety, prevent fraud and misuse, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

      • 4. Public Business Information

        Information intentionally submitted for public business discovery may appear on the T.O.E. website, magazine, social-media platforms, QR-linked pages and other promotional materials.

        This may include business name, description, services, business contact details, location, photographs, website and social-media information.

        Businesses should not submit confidential or unnecessary personal information for public publication.

      • 5. Service Provider Information

        T.O.E. may collect additional information from Service Network applicants for registration, verification and customer-safety purposes.

        Private information may include identification documents, references, guarantor information, verification records, internal incident reports and residential information.

        Such information will be restricted to authorised persons and disclosed only where there is an appropriate lawful basis.

      • 6. QR Codes, Cookies & Analytics

        T.O.E. may use QR codes, cookies, analytics tools and similar technologies to understand how users discover and interact with businesses and T.O.E. content.

        Information may include scan activity, approximate location, date and time, device information, referral source, pages visited and campaign engagement.

        Where required, we will provide appropriate cookie controls and consent mechanisms.

      • 7. Sharing Information

        We may share information where necessary with authorised T.O.E. personnel, technology and hosting providers, payment processors, analytics and marketing providers, production and distribution partners, professional advisers, relevant businesses or service providers when a user requests a connection, and government or law-enforcement authorities where legally required or appropriate.

        T.O.E. does not sell personal information simply because you use our Platform.

      • 8. Third-Party Services

        Our Platform may use or link to services such as WhatsApp, social-media platforms, payment processors, maps, analytics tools, advertising platforms and other third-party services.

        Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

      • 9. Data Security & Retention

        T.O.E. uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

        We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business purposes, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security or other lawful purposes.

      • 10. Your Privacy Rights

        Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where applicable, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, request data portability where applicable, and make a complaint regarding the handling of your information.

        To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

      • 11. Marketing

        Where permitted by law, T.O.E. may send newsletters, business opportunities, events, promotions and other relevant communications.

        You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

      • 12. Children

        T.O.E. does not intentionally collect personal information from children where parental or guardian consent is required. Where children's information is necessary for a specific service or activity, it will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

      • 13. Changes to This Policy

        T.O.E. may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, data practices or legal obligations change.

        The latest version will always be published on the website with the applicable “Last Updated” date.
