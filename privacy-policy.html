      T.O.E Guide

      How we protect your information.

      This Privacy Policy explains how T.O.E Guide collects, reviews, stores and uses information from visitors, vendors, business owners, distributors, partners and people who submit forms through our website.

      Last updated: September 2026

      1. Information We Collect

      We collect information you submit directly to T.O.E Guide, including full name, business name, category, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, resident state, work station or location, business address, social media handles, service details, photos, messages and enquiry details.

      For verification and onboarding, we may also collect documents such as NIN, driver's license, CAC documents, address proof, owner identification, business media, service photos and other supporting materials you choose to upload.

      2. How We Use Your Information

      We use submitted information to review onboarding requests, verify vendors and business owners, create or update listings, respond to enquiries, manage T.O.E Guide campaigns, prepare QR discovery records, support distributor network placement and improve the platform.

      3. Public Listing Information

      When a listing is approved, selected public details may appear on T.O.E Guide pages, QR routes, directories, blog features, event pages, social media features, campaign materials or distributor network materials. Public details may include business name, vendor name, category, location, description, images and approved contact routes.

      4. Private Verification Materials

      Verification documents are used for review and trust checks. We treat these documents as private review materials and do not intentionally publish them as listing content.

      5. Sharing and Service Providers

      We do not sell personal information. We may share approved listing information with users, distributors, campaign partners or service providers who help us operate T.O.E Guide, manage submissions, host files, process forms, maintain databases or track operations.

      6. Google Sheets, Supabase and Operations Tools

      Form submissions may be stored in T.O.E Guide databases and may also be copied into operational trackers such as Dream Digital sheets for review, follow-up and internal management.

      7. Your Choices

      You may request correction, update or removal of submitted details. Some records may be retained where required for verification, security, business records, dispute prevention or platform administration.

      8. Security

      We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect submitted information. No online platform can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid submitting information that is not required for your request.

      9. Updates to This Policy

      T.O.E Guide may update this Privacy Policy as the platform grows, as new features are added, or as legal and operational requirements change.

      10. Contact

      For privacy questions, corrections or removal requests, contact T.O.E Guide through the contact page or email hello@toeguide.com.
